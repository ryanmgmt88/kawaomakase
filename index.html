About

An intimate omakase counter in the heart of the East Village.

KAWA Omakase brings a seasonal $88 Japanese tasting menu to 37 E 1st Street in New York City's East Village. Each evening, guests are guided through a carefully curated omakase experience rooted in Japanese culinary tradition and elevated with modern artistry.

Chef Tony brings over 20 years of experience from celebrated New York restaurants including Catch NYC, Sushi Dojo, Sushi on Jones, Akimori, and Bonito 47. His omakase menu changes with the seasons, highlighting the finest fish and ingredients available each week.
