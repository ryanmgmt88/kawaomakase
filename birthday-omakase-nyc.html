Birthday Omakase Dinner in NYC

Birthday Omakase FAQ

Can I celebrate a birthday at Kawa?

Yes. Kawa Omakase is a sushi counter that can work well for intimate birthday dinners and small celebrations.

Can Kawa host private birthday dinners?

Private omakase dinners and restaurant buyouts are available by inquiry through the events page.

How many guests can a private event accommodate?

Kawa Omakase can accommodate private events of up to 21 guests, with 13 counter seats and 8 table seats.

How do I reserve a birthday dinner?

Reservations through Resy are recommended for birthday dinners. Walk-ins are welcome when seating is available, and larger private events should start with the event inquiry form.
