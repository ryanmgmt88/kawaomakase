Why Choose Kawa in the East Village?

Kawa works well for diners who want a chef-led sushi tasting in downtown Manhattan without needing to sort through a long menu or compare separate tasting formats. The 14-course structure gives first-time omakase guests a clear path through the meal, while the East Village location keeps it convenient for guests coming from NoHo, Bowery, Nolita, SoHo, NYU, Union Square, and the Lower East Side.
