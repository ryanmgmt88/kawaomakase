Date Night Omakase in NYC

Kawa Omakase is a sushi counter in Manhattan’s East Village. For date night omakase in NYC, the appeal is straightforward: a $88, 14-course chef-led tasting where the meal unfolds course by course at the counter.

The fixed-price structure helps couples plan the evening before they book, and the counter format keeps the focus on the sushi, the pacing, and the conversation around each course. Reservations through Resy are recommended to secure your preferred time.

What to Expect From the 14-Course Format

A date-night omakase at Kawa is not built around ordering several dishes for the table. Guests sit for a chef-selected progression of seasonal courses, including nigiri prepared one piece at a time. The menu changes with availability, so the experience is structured but not static.

Kawa is located at 37 East 1st Street, convenient for guests coming from nearby downtown neighborhoods including NoHo, Bowery, SoHo, Nolita, NYU, Union Square, and the Lower East Side.
