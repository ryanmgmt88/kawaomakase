Ingredients

Our Omakase Ingredients

At Kawa Omakase, selected fish and seasonal ingredients shape the $88, 14-course omakase served at the counter in Manhattan’s East Village.

Many of Kawa’s omakase ingredients are sourced from Japan, alongside selected ingredients from other regions. King salmon from New Zealand and toro from Spanish bluefin tuna are examples of ingredients that may shape the menu.

Menu14 seasonal courses
Price$88 per guest
ExamplesJapanese fish, king salmon, toro
LocationEast Village, NYC

How Ingredients Shape the Menu

Kawa’s menu changes based on seasonality, quality, and availability. That means the same 14-course format can feel different across visits because the chef-led progression follows the menu at that time.

For the full menu structure, visit the menu page. For a deeper story about sourcing and seasonality, read the journal article on seasonal fish sourcing.
